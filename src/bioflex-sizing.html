    All layouts for this digester

    The tool picks the layout with the fewest circuits, which means the fewest wall penetrations and end connectors, and then the least pipe. A single coil stays below 492 ft (150 m).

    Email this sizing to BRUGG for a quote

    Method: BRUGG design sheet for BIOFLEX digester heating. One circuit is a single-pass heat exchanger in the substrate with a linear heat transfer value of 65 W/(m·K) (37.6 BTU/(h·ft·°F)) for BIOFLEX CNW 60/66. Loop diameter is the tank diameter minus 1.3 ft (0.4 m). The result is a budget sizing for a complete mix digester; it does not replace the design check by BRUGG engineering, and it does not include the pressure loss of the heating circuit. Nothing you enter is stored or sent.
