Why BIOFLEX

Six reasons to specify it.

  • 8 htypical installation, against about 80 h for welded steel pipe

    Far less labor in the tank

    Unroll the coil, clamp the pipe to the brackets, mount the end fittings. No pipe bending, no welder, no pickling.

  • 0 weldsinside the digester

    One continuous pipe per circuit

    Made in long lengths and shipped in coils of up to 492 ft (150 m). No welded or threaded joints in the substrate.

  • +40%heat transfer area against smooth pipe

    More heat from every foot

    The helical corrugation enlarges the surface and keeps the water turbulent, which also prevents scaling.

  • 0 loopsself-compensating

    Takes up its own expansion

    The corrugated pipe absorbs thermal expansion and follows the curve of any tank wall. No expansion loops, no elbows.

  • 316Lstainless steel, 2 in (DN 50)

    Built to stay in the substrate

    Corrosion-resistant AISI 316L (1.4404), helium tested. No weld seams in the tank where corrosion could start.

  • 1,000+digesters equipped

    Engineered as one system

    BRUGG sizes the heating for your digester and supplies BIOFLEX in the tank plus CALPEX or CASAFLEX to the heat source.
