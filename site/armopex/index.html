ARMOPEX – Flexible Pipe for Medium-Pressure District Heating

Flexible, Reinforced, Pre-Insulated & Self-Compensating. Aramid-reinforced PE-Xa pipe for 174 psi at 185 °F. Unroll it into a narrow trench, bend it around what is in the way, bolt the ends. No welding on the pipe, no expansion loops.

Why ARMOPEX

Six reasons to specify it.

  • ~60%less installation labor

    Trench and labor savings

    Reference case, 400 ft of 3 in supply and return: 89 instead of 242 labor hours against rigid welded pipe, in a trench 26 in wide instead of 44 in.

  • 0 weldson the pipe

    Fast installation

    Bolted clamp fittings, installed with hand tools. No welding on the pipe and no press tool. Threaded, weld-end, coupling and T-piece versions.

  • 1,400 ftin one coil (427 m)

    Long lengths, few joints

    Coils from 120 ft in the largest size to 1,400 ft in the smallest. The pipe runs largely without joints in the ground.

  • 0 loopsself-compensating

    Self-compensating

    The bonded PE-Xa system is laid without taking thermal expansion into account: no expansion loops and no compensators.

  • 2.7 ftmin. bend radius (0.8 m)

    Bends around what is in the way

    Minimum bending radius from 2.7 ft in size 40/91 to 4.6 ft in size 125/182. Crosses over or under existing utilities.

  • USAmade, sizes 40/91 to 110/162

    Made in the USA

    Sizes 40/91 to 110/162 are manufactured in the USA. Size 125/182 is imported.

Trench and labor

About 60% less trench and labor cost than rigid welded pipe.

Reference case: 400 ft of trench, 3 in supply and return. The rigid system needs 32 field welds and 28 joint casings. ARMOPEX needs 8 clamp connections and 2 joint casings.

System

An aramid-reinforced PE-Xa pipe, insulated and jacketed in one piece.

Carrier pipePE-Xa, peroxide cross-linked HDPE
Oxygen barrierAluminum foil, elongation > 20 %
ReinforcementAramid (Kevlar) mesh with protective polypropylene film
InsulationCFC-free PUR foam, ≤ 0.14 Btu·in/(h·ft²·°F) at 122 °F (≤ 0.0199 W/m·K)
Outer jacketSeamlessly extruded LLDPE, UV-resistant
Continuous temperature185 °F (85 °C)
Peak temperature239 °F (115 °C) for 100 h
Operating pressure174 psi (12 bar)
Thermal expansionSelf-compensating
StandardsManufactured in accordance with EN 15632-1/-2 and TS 17889

Clamp connectors with steel insert, brass clamp and stainless steel bolts, installed without special tools. Also available: 90° house entry bends, pre-insulated T-connections, clip shells, connecting sockets, end caps and wall seals.

Installation

Unroll, cut, bolt. Hand tools only.

One screw fitting system for all seven sizes, 40/91 to 125/182. The narrated video follows the installation manual step by step. Installation only by trained personnel; training is offered on site or online.
