What it looks like in your digester

LoopThe pipe laid once around the tank wall. Its length is set by the digester diameter.
CircuitOne continuous coil of pipe with its own supply and return through the wall. A circuit makes one or more loops; no joints inside the tank.
RowLoops sit above each other, 10 in apart, the lowest 3 ft above the floor. One bracket holds two rows.

How much pipe you really need

Your BIOFLEX package

Brackets hold two loops each and are counted at one every 3.3 ft (1.0 m) of tank circumference, rounded up to full packs of 10. All fittings have 2 in NPT threads. The 2 in NPT hard pipe through the wall and the 90° union are supplied by the client.

    Method: BRUGG design sheet for BIOFLEX digester heating. One circuit is a single-pass heat exchanger in the substrate with a linear heat transfer value of 65 W/(m·K) (37.6 BTU/(h·ft·°F)) for BIOFLEX CNW 60/66. Loop diameter is the tank diameter minus 1.3 ft (0.4 m). The result is a budget sizing for a complete mix digester; it does not replace the design check by BRUGG engineering, and it does not include the pressure loss of the heating circuit.
