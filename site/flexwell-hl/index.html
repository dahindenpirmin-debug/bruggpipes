FLEXWELL-HL – Flexible Double-Wall Stainless Steel Pipe for Fuel Systems

Double-Wall, Continuous, Monitored & Fire-Rated. One corrugated stainless steel pipe inside another, laid in one piece. The space between the two walls is there for leak detection along the whole line.

Why FLEXWELL-HL

Six reasons to specify it.

  • 3,000 ftfactory lengths, up to 914 m

    One piece from end to end

    FLEXWELL-HL is laid directly into the trench in one piece and follows any direction, without intermediate joints or fittings.

  • 2 wallswith a continuous interstitial space

    Leak detection along the whole line

    The space between the primary and the secondary pipe is monitored by pressure or by vacuum, depending on local regulations and operating requirements.

  • 2 hfire rating per UL 1369

    Certified for above ground

    A two-hour fire rating per UL 1369. The same pipe runs below ground, above ground and on the dock.

  • 316Lstainless steel, primary and secondary pipe

    Impermeable, also for the fuels to come

    Stainless steel is an enduring, impermeable barrier, even with new fuels or fuel blends. A polyethylene jacket protects the pipe against corrosion.

  • 20 inminimum bending radius of HL 30/48 (500 mm)

    Flexible and impact resistant

    The corrugation of both pipes gives high flexibility and excellent impact resistance at the same time. The pipe follows the route, also where the route is not straight.

  • 17 fttidal range on floating docks (about 5 m)

    Made for marinas

    A continuous line from the shore to the floating dock, with no connections over water and no additional components for tidal differences of up to about 17 ft.

Applications

One pipe for below ground, above ground and marinas.

FLEXWELL-HL is designed for hazardous fluids and gases. It works as a suction pipe, a pressure pipe or a fill pipe.

Gas stationsUnderground suction, pressure and fill lines between tank and dispenser.
MarinasFuel lines to floating docks, with no connections over water.
Bulk facilities and generatorsAbove ground fuel lines at tank farms and generator tanks, fire rated per UL 1369.
Chemical plants and LPGHazardous fluids typically transported in chemical plants, and LPG systems.

Closed mechanical end fitting

The end fitting closes the pipe and connects it to the system. It is available with NPT thread or as EZ-Fit.

Leak detection

The interstitial space between the primary and the secondary pipe is designed for leak detection by pressure or by vacuum, with suitable and approved leak detection systems. Per the data sheet, UL 1369 does not require constant leak monitoring for FLEXWELL-HL; local regulations decide.

Range

Four sizes, from 1 in to 3 in.

All sizes share the same construction and the same limits: 145 psig (10 bar) in the primary pipe, suction operation under vacuum, and monitoring of the secondary pipe at −8.7 psi (−0.6 bar) vacuum or up to 145 psig (10 bar) pressure.
