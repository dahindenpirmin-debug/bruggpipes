System

A steel pipe, insulated and jacketed as one bonded unit.

Service pipeWelded steel, grade P235GH, to EN 253
InsulationPolyurethane (PUR) foam, three insulation series
Jacket pipePE-HD to EN 253
Leak detectionMonitoring wires in the foam, Nordic or Brandes
Continuous temperature, UNO291 °F (144 °C)
Continuous temperature, DUO266 °F (130 °C)
Operating pressure362 psi (25 bar)*
Thermal expansionTaken up by expansion bends and expansion pads; pipe statics by BRUGG engineering
Field jointDouble-sealing shrink joint casing, or INDUCON welded casing
StandardsEN 253, EN 448, EN 488, EN 489, EN 13941; DUO to EN 15698

* Not valid for all temperature and diameter combinations. Higher temperatures on request.

Range

22 single-pipe sizes, 11 twin-pipe sizes.

Each size in up to three insulation series. Pipe is made to metric dimensions; metric values govern. Steel wall thicknesses follow EN 253, not ASME schedules.

Welded joint

INDUCON: the joint casing welded by induction.

For groundwater, pressurized water and difficult ground. A stainless steel mesh band is heated without contact and welds the joint casing to the jacket all the way round. No wires leave the joint, so there is no path for moisture.

Watch the INDUCON video on YouTubeINDUCON brochure (PDF)Joints data sheet (PDF)

The video opens on youtube.com in a new tab. Casing pipe sizes 3.54 to 27.95 in (90 to 710 mm). Welding equipment: 230 V / 16 A, run from a standard generator. Installers need FW 603 or comparable certification and INDUCON training; BRUGG trains your crew if needed.
