Why CASAFLEX

Six reasons to specify it.

  • −70%trench and labor cost

    Narrower trench, far less labor

    On 375 ft of 3" supply and return, the trench is 26 in wide instead of 44 in. No field welds, no joint casings.

  • 1 dayinstead of about a week

    Fast installation

    Unrolled straight from the coil and connected with bolted fittings and hand tools. The trench closes the same day.

  • 1,509 ftin one piece

    Long lengths, few joints

    From 157 ft in 4" up to 1,509 ft in ¾", shipped in a 40' HC container. No joints buried along the run.

  • 0 loopsself-compensating

    No expansion loops, no anchors

    The corrugated carrier pipe takes up thermal expansion by itself. No expansion pads, no pipe statics for expansion.

  • 3.3 ftminimum bending radius

    Bends around what is in the way

    Passes over or under existing utilities and follows the shortest route, without a single elbow to weld and insulate.

  • 302 °Fcontinuous, 362 psi

    High temperature and steam condensate

    Peaks up to 356 °F (180 °C), rated PN 16 and PN 25. Steam condensate return with AISI 316L carrier pipe, proven in reference projects.

Trench and labor

About 70% less trench and labor cost than rigid welded pipe.

Reference case: 375 ft of trench, 3" supply and return. The rigid system needs 30 field welds and 26 joint casings. CASAFLEX needs neither.

System

A corrugated stainless steel pipe, insulated and jacketed in one piece.

Carrier pipeHelically corrugated stainless steel, AISI 304 or AISI 316L
InsulationCFC-free PIR foam with barrier film
ReinforcementExpanded metal mesh
Outer jacketCorrugated PE-LD, seamlessly extruded
Leak detectionThree monitoring wires, Nordic or Brandes
Continuous temperature302 °F (150 °C)
Peak temperature356 °F (180 °C)
Operating pressure232 psi (PN 16) or 362 psi (PN 25)
Thermal expansionSelf-compensating
Steam condensateWith AISI 316L carrier pipe
StandardsEN 15632-4, EN 10088, EN 253
